**The Gilded Vault** — a top-down stealth heist in a museum at midnight. You are the thief: slip past three patrolling guards and two sweeping cameras, pocket the Golden Scarab, the Jade Mask and the Star Sapphire, crack the vault, grab the Cache and slip out the exit door. Get seen and the alarm climbs; let it top out and you burn one of three attempts. Play it clean — never let the alarm pass 50 — and the ghost bonus is yours. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080/ in a desktop browser.

WASD or arrow keys move, Shift sneaks (slower, and the alarm rises about half as fast while you are seen), hold Space beside a prize to pocket it, P or Escape pauses, M mutes, Space or Enter or a click starts and confirms. Best score is kept in the browser between visits.
